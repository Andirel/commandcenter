# 06 — End-of-day brief

**Phase 5 · Posts to internal Slack only**

A short close-out. Deliberately much smaller than the morning brief: the day is
over, and the only useful content is what changed and what is now at risk.

## Trigger

Weekday evenings, on a schedule.

## Content

- What completed today (with the evidence that established it)
- What became overdue
- What needs a decision before tomorrow morning
- Anything that arrived late and materially changed priorities

## Rule

If nothing material happened, send **one line** saying so. A daily ritual that
manufactures content to justify itself stops being read within two weeks.
