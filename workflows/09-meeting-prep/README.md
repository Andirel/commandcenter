# 09 — Meeting prep

**Phase 4 · Posts to internal Slack only**

Assembles what an informed person would want before walking into a meeting.

## Trigger

Scheduled, ahead of each sufficiently important calendar event. Importance is
derived from attendees, the organizations involved, and related open work — not
every meeting warrants a brief.

## Retrieved context

- Participants and their organizations
- Previous mail threads with these people
- Previous meetings on this subject
- Open tasks involving them
- Outstanding commitments **in both directions**
- Recent decisions
- Relevant Drive documents (Phase 8)
- What each internal person has been doing on it

## Brief structure

```
MEETING
OBJECTIVE
WHAT WE NEED FROM THEM
WHAT THEY NEED FROM US
THEY OWE US
WE OWE THEM
OPEN DECISIONS
WHO ON OUR TEAM IS INVOLVED
WHAT CHANGED
RECOMMENDED QUESTIONS
```

"They owe us" and "we owe them" are the sections that most change how a meeting
goes, and they are exactly what nobody has time to reconstruct beforehand.

## Rule

If there is genuinely no context, say so in one line. A prep brief that pads
empty sections is worse than none — it costs reading time and teaches the reader
that prep briefs are not worth opening.
